Clipper

A personal tool for making and posting short-form clips for creator clipping campaigns.

What it does

Clipper is run by Avinaash Ramasamy for their own accounts. It finds moments in creators' streams and long videos (only for creators whose clipping campaigns Avinaash Ramasamy has joined), edits them into vertical clips, and posts them to Avinaash Ramasamy's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. The links to posted clips are kept so they can be submitted to the campaign for payout.

Who can use it

Clipper is not a public service. It runs on its owner's computer and only signs in to accounts its owner controls. There is no sign-up and no other users.
