Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Clipper is a personal tool operated by Avinaash Ramasamy ("we"). It is used only with accounts we own. This policy explains what data Clipper accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how to revoke access.

YouTube API Services

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using Clipper you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data Clipper accesses

  • Your connected accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram): the account or channel name and ID, and permission to upload videos. Clipper reads your channel information only to confirm which channel it is posting to.
  • Public data: public information about creators and videos (such as titles, view counts and publish dates) from YouTube, Twitch and other platforms, used to decide which clips to make.
  • Sign-in tokens: access and refresh tokens issued by each platform when you sign in.

How the data is used

Data is used only to make clips and upload them to your own accounts, and to keep a record of the links to clips we posted so they can be submitted to clipping campaigns. Clipper does not use the data for advertising, does not build profiles of viewers, and does not sell or rent any data.

Storage

Everything is stored on the operator's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in files readable only by the operator's user account. Clipper does not run a server and does not store data in the cloud. Public statistics are kept only as long as needed to choose clips and are refreshed regularly.

Sharing

Clipper does not share data with third parties. The only information that leaves the operator's computer is: videos uploaded to the operator's own accounts, links to those videos submitted to the relevant clipping campaign, and text sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to help choose and caption clips. No account tokens or personal data are sent to that service.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Clipper itself does not place cookies on any device.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Clipper's access to a Google account at any time from the Google security settings page. TikTok and Instagram access can be removed in each app's settings under connected or authorised apps. Deleting the local token files removes Clipper's stored sign-ins; on request we delete any stored data within 7 days.
